2026

  • Improving NaNDA Discoverability: Removing Barriers from a Research Gem
    Lucas Rosendall
    An interrogation of how neighborhood-level data should be described and represented for easier discovery and reuse.
    pdf presentation
    The National Neighborhood Data Archive (NaNDA) is a publicly available data archive containing measures of the physical, economic, demographic, and social environment at multiple levels of spatial scale. We want to know how metadata affects the ways in which users access and experience the NaNDA repository. Our research is framed around the understanding that Computational Social Scientists have unique information needs that require specialized resources to conduct research and scholarship. This project then seeks to address the obstacles and challenges that come with designing metadata systems that support specialized scholarly-work in the social sciences.
  • The Negative Library of Other Networks: Permacomputing & The Cozy Web
    Lucas Rosendall
    A Permacomputing Web Archive that addresses appraisal, collection settings, quality assurance, & metadata description.
    pdf
    The scope of this project prioritizes blogs & web histories dedicated to alternative visions for the internet. More specifically, this project focuses on cozy web or poetic web perspectives of ecology, namely permacomputing, feral web, and low-tech horizons. The report documents the appraisal criteria used to acquire relevant material and maintain the collection’s scope; the ideal capture settings of the collection; Quality Assurance workflow and related procedures; and lastly, diagramatic depictions of the Web Archiving workflow along with documentation policies related to copyright and fair usem, or intellectual property overall.
  • Unifying the Detroit Future City (DFC) Photo Archive
    Lucas Rosendall
    This report outlines short term and long term digital preservation considerations for a client project.
    pdf
    This report finalizes short term and long term preservation considerations and DAM tool recommendations for Detroit Future City (DFC) – a nonprofit organization specializing in land and economic development, neighborhood planning, and economic inclusion
